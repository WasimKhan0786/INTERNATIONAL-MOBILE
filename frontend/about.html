Our Vision

Welcome to INTERNATIONAL MOBILE, your ultimate destination for everything mobile accessories. We specialize in providing the finest selection of shockproof bumper covers, liquid silicone cases, 11D curved tempered glasses, high-output fast chargers, tough braided data cables, and audio adapters.

At INTERNATIONAL MOBILE, quality is our priority. We understand how valuable your smartphone is to you, which is why we curate products from trusted brands like Spigen, boAt, OnePlus, Realme, and Anker to guarantee reliable defense, utility, and power performance.

Shipping & Delivery

We currently support Cash on Delivery (COD) across the region. Orders are usually dispatched within 24 hours of placement. Delivery takes 3-5 business days depending on your delivery location. Shipping rates are calculated dynamically at checkout: flat shipping applies, which is automatically waived once your basket crosses the free shipping threshold.

Returns & Exchanges

We stand behind our accessories. If you receive a damaged, incorrect, or defective item, please contact our support team on WhatsApp within 7 days of receiving the order. We will initiate an exchange or arrange refund collection at your address immediately with no extra charges.

Privacy & Terms

INTERNATIONAL MOBILE respects your privacy. The information you provide at checkout (name, contact numbers, email, delivery address) is used strictly to process orders and handle deliveries. We never sell, rent, or lease customer directories to third-party marketing companies.
